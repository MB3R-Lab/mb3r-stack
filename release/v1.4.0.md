# mb3r-stack v1.4.0 candidate bundle

This candidate pins published Bering v1.2.0 and Sheaft v1.3.2, plus the exact
Procrustes source revision in `compat/stack-manifest.json`. It keeps the Bering
model/snapshot 1.3.0 contracts and existing adapter envelopes.

The chart exposes `bering.config.runtime.publicationMode`, defaulting to
`windowed`, and `bering.config.runtime.reconciliation.enabled`, defaulting to
`true`. Opt-in `on_shutdown` publication requires setting reconciliation to
`false`; the external orchestrator must allow the final publication to finish.
The existing continuous profiles retain their windowed behavior.

Integration checks passed in [Actions run 40798522544](https://github.com/MB3R-Lab/mb3r-stack/actions/runs/40798522544)
at revision `45ae9bc24e882cb9dbddac4342a8355b787a8921`, including the native and
declared OBI source handoff into Sheaft, adapter checks, pinned-image startup,
chart rendering, packaging and release dry run. The historical v1.3.0 bundle
retains its own pins and evidence. Publication of v1.4.0 remains pending.
